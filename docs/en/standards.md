The [Brazilian Digital Terrestrial Television Forum](http://www.forumsbtvd.org.br) has published with ABNT the standard series for the Brazilian DTV System. The SBTVD Forum organizes the standards into a table available [here](https://forumsbtvd.org.br/legislacao-e-normas-tecnicas/normas-tecnicas-da-tv-digital/english/).
